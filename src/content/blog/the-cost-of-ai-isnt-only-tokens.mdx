Had a chat with Andres Figueira on [The Front Lines](https://frontlines.io) about the cost of AI. Not just the cost of tokens, but the potential cost to your image and brand when you cut corners.

Here are 8 things I believe:

## 1. The tools are the easy part.

Making the human + the system work is where it’s won or lost.

## 2. The people who resist need a culture, not to be strong-armed.

Let them start small. Hear the fears. And sometimes they’re right: some texts shouldn’t be written by an AI.

## 3. Heavy users have the opposite problem.

They stop feeling accountable for what the AI produces. But there’s one rule: if AI made it and you submit it, your name is on it.

## 4. Wrong assumptions spread everywhere.

AI invents a product capability... and boom, it’s everywhere: collateral, decks, documents. A customer wants to buy it and your pre-sales tells them it doesn’t exist. Who looks like a 🤡 (and loses the sale)?

## 5. Review is the new bottleneck.

AI writes 30 pages (repeating itself 5x). Your one expert can’t check them all. Train people to write shorter and validate the key points.

## 6. Token budgets are the wrong first question.

Ask: for this task, how much am I ready to invest, and what’s the cheapest way to do it well?

## 7. AI can keep the unsaid unsaid.

It doesn’t challenge the leader. Without the safety to contradict, it becomes confirmation bias, at scale. “Listen to me, I’m right, Claude said so.”

## 8. Who pays the bill? Your brand, your credibility.

The moment people think it’s AI, you lose credibility. We still want to do business with humans.
